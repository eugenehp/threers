examples

Left, THREE.Fur: shell fur on a ball swinging on a pendulum — the coat trails by its inertia and by the drag of the air it moves through, swings past and settles; gusts are turbulence carried downwind at the wind's speed. Middle, THREE.Hair: 16,000 strands hanging from a head. Right, curly clumped fur on a torus. All of it casts and receives shadows, and under MSAA each sample is tested against the strands. Move the pointer over a coat to stroke it — the strands follow your hand and part around it, then spring back. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
