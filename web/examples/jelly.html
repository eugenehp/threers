Grab a tentacle, an arm, a tail or an ear and pull until it gives: it twitches on the table while a bud swells from the stump. Press the body, throw the goo drops, scroll while holding to twist. Drag the table to orbit.

Built on THREE.SoftBody (threers::softbody, XPBD in wasm) and THREE.SDF: each creature is a few distance fields, meshed by surface nets. Drawn by threers: the gel is a MeshPhysicalMaterial in refract mode, thickness measured from its back faces, with eyes, gonads and bellies as opaque surfaces inside it.
