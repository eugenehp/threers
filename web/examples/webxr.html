A table of shapes to look at in a headset. Point a controller (or a hand) at a shape to light it up, pull the trigger (or pinch) to recolour it, and hold the grip to pick it up and move it. On Apple Vision Pro, look at a shape and pinch: a quick pinch recolours it, and pinching and holding carries it. Two candles burn at the front of the table (THREE.Candle, threers' own: simple on the left, volumetric on the right). In AR the room shows through and the floor is hidden. On a desktop, drag to look around. The page uses three.js's renderer.xr, VRButton and ARButton. Headsets only allow WebXR on a secure page (https), and the wasm build needs the xr feature (XR=1 ./web/build.sh).
