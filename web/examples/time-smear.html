The scene is rendered once by threers, then every pixel's brightness over 64-frame windows is sent through a Gray-coded interference transform (threers::time_smear, the same Rust as native, in wasm). Moving things echo at specific frames on both sides of where they are: the pink box is only there for frames 70–85, yet its ghost arrives early. The backdrop never changes, so it is left exactly alone. Gain 0 means the look's own default.
