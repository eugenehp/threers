Every material below is rendered live in this page by the threers wasm/wgpu renderer — the presets from materials::presets, plus the extended MeshPhysicalMaterial layers. The environment cubemap and the foil crinkle normal map are generated procedurally in JavaScript, so nothing is downloaded. Note that the metals need scene.environment: with no environment a metalness = 1 sphere has no diffuse term and renders near-black no matter how many lights you add.
